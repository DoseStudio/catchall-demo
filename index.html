Questions people ask

How far ahead should I book?

Weddings book 6 to 12 months out. Smaller events can often be done in a few weeks, so send your date and ask.

Do you travel outside Kansas City?

Yes. Anything within about an hour is included. Farther than that adds a travel fee, which we put in your quote.

What does a quote include?

Bartender hours, setup and breakdown, the bar itself, and a drink menu. Glassware and extra staff are listed as add-ons.

How soon will I hear back?

You get a confirmation email as soon as you send the form, and a personal reply within one business day.
